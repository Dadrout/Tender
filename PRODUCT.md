# QazBid AI
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
User-specified Next.js App Router, TypeScript, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, SQLite, Python 3.12+, optional Google Gemini.
## Users
SMEs in Taraz and Zhambyl, especially stores supplying household and construction materials. Hackathon jury evaluating a 90-second workflow.
## Product Purpose
Assess ability to fulfill a tender and economic profitability before participation, from real PDF and inventory evidence.
## Positioning
Requirement-level evidence and deterministic financial calculations; semantic similarity never proves compliance.
## Operating Context
Russian default, Kazakh secondary, KZT, local development without external credentials. Reusable catalogs and saved analyses.
## Capabilities and Constraints
PDF extraction, inventory imports, source references, manual corrections, requirements matrix, scenarios, risks, reports, persistence. Synthetic examples must be labeled; unknown facts stay unknown. No live procurement access without configured authorization.
## Brand Commitments
Premium enterprise FinTech clarity inspired by Linear, Stripe, Vercel, Ramp and Attio. Restrained colors, accessible tables, dark and light themes. No fake controls or claims.
## Evidence on Hand
User supplied a detailed build brief. No real customer claims or live procurement dataset. Demo documents will be generated and labeled synthetic.
## Product Principles
Evidence before decisions. Explicit assumptions. Decimal arithmetic. Honest processing mode. Preserve user corrections.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Responsive down to 390px. Labeled controls and keyboard-accessible navigation.
## Delegated choices
Local stack delivery; hosted production auth remains a documented deployment prerequisite. Implementation starts directly as explicitly requested by the user.
